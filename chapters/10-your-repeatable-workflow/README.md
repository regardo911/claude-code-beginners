# Chapter 10: Your Repeatable Workflow

The distilled version of Chapters 2 through 9, on one page you can tape next to your
monitor. `daily-checklist.md` is that page, five sections, exactly as the book prints it.

Open the checklist, screenshot it to your phone or print it, use it tomorrow. The three
prompts underneath are the ones the routine assumes: the morning status check, `git status`
before you start, and the end-of-day summary.

Done when you've run one full day against it (morning check, one feature built, tests run,
end-of-day summary) and it felt like a routine rather than a form.

The prompt library the chapter also builds is in `starter-kit/prompts.md`. Five templates
with the brackets left in: you fill them when you paste, which is what keeps each one under
30 words.

Customize the checklist. Add items, cut ones that don't apply. Every line traces back to a
chapter that explains why it's there.
